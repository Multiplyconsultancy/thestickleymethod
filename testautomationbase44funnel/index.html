Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this actually free?

Everything from me is free: the course, the calls, the community and the bonuses. The only thing you pay for is the platform your app is built on, and that goes to them directly.

What is the platform, and why do I need it?

Think ChatGPT, except instead of talking back to you it builds real working software. You describe what you want in plain English and it builds it. Your app lives on it, so you need an account.

Why do I have to use an incognito window?

It’s what lets the partnership see that you came from me. A normal browser window can carry old cookies that stop your sign-up being credited, and an uncredited sign-up can’t unlock your access.

Why the Builder plan and not Starter?

Starter can’t build what the course builds, so you’d get stuck in the first few videos. Builder is the plan the whole course is made for.

I already have an account on the platform. Can I still claim?

Answer to confirm with the partner

What if I got a step wrong?

Don’t make a second account. Message support below with what happened and we’ll get it sorted.

Do I need any tech skills?

None. You build it by typing in plain English. If you can send a text, you can build it.

How long do I get the weekly calls for?

They’re not capped. You keep access for as long as the partnership is active.

How old do I need to be?

16 or over.
