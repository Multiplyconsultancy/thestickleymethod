No. If you are starting from zero, bring that to the first call and you will leave knowing where to start. The system is where you keep yourself honest once you do.

No. Everything we work on is what you can do yourself. This is habits, sequencing and consistency, not procedures.

Your photos and your answers sit in your own account under your login. It is your app, not mine. Nobody is looking at your photos but you.

Honestly, I do not know. The partnership covers it right now and that can change. When it does, this goes back to what it used to sell for.

They are not capped. You get access for as long as the partnership is active.
